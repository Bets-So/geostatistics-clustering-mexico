# Data

This project uses publicly available data. Raw files are not included in this
repository due to their size; follow the steps below to download them.

## 1. Registered Deaths Statistics (2014–2018)

- **Source:** National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI),
  *Estadísticas de Defunciones Registradas (EDR)*, microdata.
- **Link:** https://www.inegi.org.mx/programas/edr/#microdatos
- **Years used:** 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017 and 2018.
- **Format:** DBF files, one per year.
- **Accessed:** 2015 - 2019

### How to download

1. Go to the link above and open the **Microdatos** section.
2. Download the files for period 2015 - 2019.
3. Extract the death records file for each year (`DEFUN14.dbf` … `DEFUN18.dbf`).
4. Save them in `data/raw/Defunciones/`.

## 2. Population projections and denominators

- **Source:** National Population Council (CONAPO), population projections
  for states and municipalities, by sex and 5-year age groups.
- **Edition used:** 2016
- **Accessed:** 2020
- **Used for:** denominators of age-standardized rates (reference year: 2016).
- **Files:** archived in `data/raw/Proyecciones/` exactly as originally downloaded.

> **Note:** CONAPO has since published a new edition of municipal projections
> (2021–2040), and the original files are no longer available in the same format.
> They are archived in this repository to keep the analysis reproducible.
> Newer editions may produce different rates.
>
Used as denominators of age-standardized rates (reference year: 2016),
by sex and 5-year age groups.

### State level

- **Source:** CONAPO (2023, August 4). *Conciliación Demográfica de 1950 a 2019
  y Proyecciones de la población de México y de las entidades federativas
  2020 a 2070*.
  https://www.gob.mx/conapo/acciones-y-programas/conciliacion-demografica-de-1950-a-2019-y-proyecciones-de-la-poblacion-de-mexico-y-de-las-entidades-federativas-2020-a-2070
- **Values used:** 2016 estimates from the demographic reconciliation (1950–2019).

### Municipal level

- **Source:** National Population Council (CONAPO), population projections
  for states and municipalities, by sex and 5-year age groups.
- **Values used:** 2016, by sex and 5-year age groups.

### Archived files

The original files are archived in `data/raw/Proyecciones/` exactly as
downloaded, since CONAPO has changed how this information is published.
Current data: https://www.datos.gob.mx/dataset/proyecciones-de-poblacion

> Current data: https://www.datos.gob.mx/dataset/proyecciones-de-poblacion


## 3. Municipal boundaries

- **Source:** National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEGI),
  *Marco Geoestadístico* 2016.
- **File:** `MUNICIPIOS.shp`, provided by the thesis advisor.
- **Join key:** `CVE_ENT` + `CVE_MUN` (5-digit municipal code).
- **Link:** https://www.inegi.org.mx/temas/mg/
